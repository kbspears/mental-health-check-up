Take a couple of minutes to check in. Think about the past two weeks. There's no judgment and no wrong answers.

How to answer

For each question, select how often you've experienced the concern over the past two weeks.

Your 10 questions

Choose one answer for every question, then select “See my results.”

1.Have you had trouble falling asleep, staying asleep, or getting restful sleep?
2.Have you felt tired, drained, or low on energy even after resting?
3.Have you had trouble concentrating, thinking clearly, or making decisions?
4.Have everyday responsibilities felt more overwhelming than usual?
5.Have you felt tense, on edge, or unable to switch off your mind?
6.Have you found yourself getting irritated or frustrated over small things?
7.Have you snapped at people or had a shorter fuse than usual?
8.Have you struggled to manage anger once it starts building?
9.Have you kept your feelings to yourself instead of talking to someone you trust?
10.Have stress, frustration, or anger interfered with your relationships, work, or enjoyment of life?

Please answer all 10 questions to see your result.

Your result

This is an informal wellness self-check, not a validated diagnostic instrument. The score bands are campaign-created guidance, not clinical cutoffs. A low score does not mean you should ignore concerns that matter to you.

Need immediate support in the U.S.? Call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If there is immediate danger, contact emergency services.
